## What is Doculent?

Doculent is an AI-powered intelligent document processing platform that eliminates manual data entry with tri-layer contextual intelligence, backed by human-in-the-loop verification. We turn fragmented files into finished business outcomes — accurately and in real-time.

**Built for high-volume operations teams** in insurance, healthcare, transportation, and freight.

### Capabilities

- **Tri-layer contextual intelligence** — domain knowledge, tenant-specific understanding, and job-level document extraction
- **99x faster** than traditional processing, averaging 20 seconds per document
- **200 documents per minute** ingestion capacity with 99.2% extraction accuracy
- **20+ integrations** for your existing tech stack
- **Human-in-the-loop** — advanced AI paired with expert human oversight
- **Zero-trust architecture** — stateless AI, HMAC-signed webhooks, cryptographic security

### Compliance

GDPR &bull; CCPA &bull; SOC 2 Type II &bull; ETSI

## How to use this repo

### Report a bug

Found something broken? [Open a bug report](https://github.com/doculent/community/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) with steps to reproduce and we'll investigate.

### Request a feature

Have an idea that would make Doculent better? [Submit a feature request](https://github.com/doculent/community/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml) and tell us about the problem you're trying to solve.

### Ask a question

Head to [Discussions](https://github.com/doculent/community/discussions) for Q&A, tips, and general conversation with the team and other users.

### Stay updated

Watch this repo or join our [Discord](https://discord.gg/VvqKKm2Ttz) for announcements, release notes, and community updates.
